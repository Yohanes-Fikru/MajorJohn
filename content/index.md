### What I Do

I currently split my time between three core areas:

*   **Leading & Managing Projects:** As a project manager with over 3 years of experience, I specialize in taking projects from concept to completion. I handle everything from requirement gathering and client communication to managing development workflows and ensuring on-time delivery. I thrive on steering teams to build robust, high-quality software.

*   **Developing Games & Technology:** I am the co-founder of Muqecha Studios, where we create games with a message. Our game, *Lomi*, is a third-person survival game that tackles the issue of street harassment. I have hands-on experience in Unity, C#, AI-driven applications, and system integrations.

*   **Building Communities:** I believe in the power of shared knowledge. I founded the Computer Science and Engineering Club (CSEC) at my university, and I continue to organize and participate in game jams and developer meetups to help grow the game development community in Ethiopia. I also created the Game Dev Academy, an initiative to teach and foster new talent.
