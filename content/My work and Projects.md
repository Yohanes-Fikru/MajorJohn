### Lomi

- **Studio:** Muqecha Studios (Co-founder)
- **Genre:** 3rd Person Action / Survival
- **Platform:** Android

**Synopsis:** Lomi is a mobile game that addresses the culture of cat-calling and street harassment. The game follows Hilaria, a young woman navigating the Ethiopian holiday of 'Timket,' who must survive bachelors pursuing her.

**My Role & Contribution:** As the co-founder of Muqecha and the Lead Developer on this project, I was responsible for the core game development, including implementing combat and enemy AI systems. I also led the project from a conceptual stage, focusing on creating a game that "sparks conversation."

### Bit-Racer

- **Company:** Excellerent Solutions
- **Genre:** Online Multiplayer / Edutainment
- **Platform:** iOS & Android

**Synopsis:** Bit-Racer is a competitive mobile multiplayer game where players race against each other by correctly typing prompts or answering trivia questions faster than their opponents.

**My Role & Contribution:** I served as the **Team Lead and Project Manager**. My responsibilities were comprehensive, including:

- Leading the project development for a remote, cross-platform team.
- Interfacing directly with the client to define requirements.
- Implementing core systems and overseeing UI redesign.
- Managing QA processes and deploying the app to both the Play Store (Android) and TestFlight (iOS).
